# Geotechnical freelance collaboration

Working notes on getting an offshore geotechnical freelancer (family) back into paid work during the 2025–26 offshore-wind downturn, and what Laminar Flow can build around him.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md) · [PLAYBOOK.pdf](PLAYBOOK.pdf) · [PLAYBOOK.html](PLAYBOOK.html) | **The compiled document.** Summary, what his freelancing is and what each job pays, where the soil work is in 2026, nine offers, directions to explore, the 30-day plan, constraints, and appendices (outreach kit, priority targets, later product options) |
| [job_acquisition_plan.md](job_acquisition_plan.md) | Long-form plan with full source links |
| [collaboration_assessment.md](collaboration_assessment.md) | Longer-term product options, ranked, deferred until he is billing |
| [outreach_kit.md](outreach_kit.md) | Three CV framings, capability statement, LinkedIn rewrite, message templates, registration links |
| [target_list.csv](target_list.csv) | 104 target accounts by segment with why-now, contact role, channel and offer fit |
| [opportunity_radar.py](opportunity_radar.py) | Daily digest of geotechnical work triggers from seventeen feeds. `python3 opportunity_radar.py --days 14` |
| [build_playbook.py](build_playbook.py) | Rebuilds PLAYBOOK.md/.html from `playbook_body.md`, `outreach_kit.md`, `target_list.csv`, `appendix_c.md`; print to PDF with headless Chromium |
