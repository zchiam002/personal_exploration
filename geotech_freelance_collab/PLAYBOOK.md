## 1. Summary

His freelance market has narrowed to offshore energy, and offshore wind, the busiest part of it since 2021, is in a documented slump until at least 2027. His skills are wider than that market. In 2026 the busiest buyers of soil-investigation, foundation-analysis, instrumentation and laboratory skills in his region are onshore and nearshore construction in Singapore and Johor, Malaysian carbon-capture and decommissioning programmes, Australian decommissioning and infrastructure, and the dispute and review market.

The plan has three layers:

1. **Income in weeks.** Expert-network calls, agency-placed rotations (offshore and onshore), and trigger-based direct outreach to companies that have just won work.
2. **Volume in months.** Singapore and Johor construction, where he presents as a site-investigation, instrumentation and lab engineer rather than an offshore specialist.
3. **Structure in a year.** Professional Engineer registration in Singapore, an expert-witness listing, and two or three niches (floating-solar anchoring, decommissioning positioning) where his rig-move history is rare.

Laminar Flow's part is to make his outreach specific and timely: a daily radar of contract awards and campaign starts, a named target list, three CV framings, a capability statement with fixed prices, and drafted messages that reference the trigger. He does the calls and the engineering. Product ideas wait until he is billing again.

## 2. What his freelancing is, and what a job pays

Clients hire him by the day or by the job to find out what the ground will do under a structure. Four kinds of work:

- **Site-investigation supervision.** On a drilling vessel or rig as the client's representative, checking that the contractor drills, samples and tests correctly, and signing off the data. Most of his income since 2018.
- **Analysis.** Whether a jack-up rig's legs will sink safely (leg penetration to ISO 19905-1), how deep piles must go and whether they can be driven, pile ageing, mudmat stability for subsea structures, conductor setting depth.
- **Reporting and review.** Factual and interpretive reports; third-party QC of other engineers' work.
- **Project management and tendering.** Pricing and running soil-investigation contracts (FOSTA), and a PUB sewer-rehabilitation contract (Smitech).

## 5. Directions to explore beyond 60 days

1. **Professional Engineer (Civil) registration in Singapore, then Specialist PE (Geotechnical).** PEB's April 2026 guidelines list SI and testing, pile load testing and interpretation, and instrumentation and monitoring as the core competencies, which he has. It converts him from engineer to signatory, roughly S$600 to S$1,500 a day. Low cost; 12–24 months.
2. **Expert witness and forensic geotechnics.** SIAC, SICC and SOP-Act adjudications in Singapore; AIAC in Malaysia (new 2026 rules); insurers' loss adjusters on settlement, slope and foundation claims. Entry: directory listings, two short forensic case notes, an expert-witness course.
3. **Floating-solar anchoring for SEA.** Malaysia's LSS6, Kenyir and Chereh, and Indonesia's reservoir programmes need lakebed SI and anchor design in soft soils. Offshore mudmat and anchor thinking applied to reservoirs; few in the region have both.
4. **Decommissioning positioning.** Malaysia's 31 platforms and Bass Strait need jack-up and heavy-lift assessments next to old, sometimes scoured foundations.
5. **Data-centre foundations in Johor.** A standing relationship with two or three piling contractors and one owner's engineer.
6. **Australia.** Engineers Australia (CPEng or NER) registration, remote contracts first, sponsorship later.
7. **Training and lecturing.** BCA Academy, polytechnics, NTU part-time modules. Low pay, high credibility.
8. **Lab accreditation consulting.** Regional SI contractors need ISO/IEC 17025 scope to bid public work.
9. **Own analysis products** once he is billing: a productized leg-penetration assessment and report automation (Appendix C).

## 8. Constraints

- **Onshore Singapore work wants a PE or a PE-supervised role.** Without PE he is hired as engineer, not signatory; fine for cash flow and the argument for §5.1.
- **Local content** in Malaysia and Indonesia favours nationals. His Malaysia play is remote analysis packages and Johor site work reachable from Singapore.
- **Rate reset.** Onshore day rates are lower than offshore; the fixed-price menu and the PE route are how he climbs back.
- **Competition** from engineers Fugro is releasing. Specificity and speed of outreach are the edge.
- **Liability and confidentiality.** Signed reports are his; he should confirm professional-indemnity cover before the first fixed-price job. Past reports belong to clients and are used only anonymised or with permission.
- **He must send the messages.** Confirm in week 1.
