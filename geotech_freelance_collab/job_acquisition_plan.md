## 1. Verdict

**His skills are wider than the market he has been selling them to.** Since 2018 he has sold "offshore geotechnical client rep" into offshore wind and oil and gas, and that market is in a slump. But the same person is a soil-investigation engineer, a laboratory-data specialist, a jack-up and pile analyst, a project manager who ran a PUB contract, and a leader who ran a Commando platoon. In 2026 the busiest buyers of those skills in his region are **onshore and nearshore construction in Singapore and Johor, Malaysian CCS and decommissioning, Australian decommissioning and infrastructure, and the dispute and review market**, not wind farms.

The fastest money is still expert-network calls and agency-placed rotations (weeks). The largest pool of work is onshore Singapore construction, which is short of engineers and paying 10–20 % more than 2024, but needs him to present as a site-investigation and instrumentation engineer, not as an offshore specialist. The most durable move is registering as a Professional Engineer in Singapore, which unlocks the roles that require a signature.

## 2. Where the work is (evidence)

### 2.1 Singapore onshore and nearshore construction
- Construction demand forecast S$32–38 bn for 2026; Changi Terminal 5 substructure and airside packages (~USD 3.56 bn awarded, tunnels under the airfield to 2029); Cross Island Line Phase 2 under construction (six underground stations, to 2032); Tuas Port later phases; HDB programme. Industry reports a ~15 % manpower shortfall, higher for engineers, with hard-to-fill roles paying 10–20 % above 2024.
- Long Island preparatory works (800 ha) from end-2026; Pulau Tekong 800 ha polder; Northern Tuas Basin reclamation instrumentation; East Coast coastal protection. Nearshore SI, ground-improvement QC, settlement monitoring and instrumentation interpretation are exactly what reclamation contractors and PUB's consultants hire.
- Public tenders for soil-investigation term contracts (HDB, LTA, JTC, PUB) are on GeBIZ; he cannot bid, but the winning SI contractors staff up after award.

**Roles he fits:** SI supervising engineer, instrumentation and monitoring engineer, ground-improvement QC, laboratory data reviewer, tender-support engineer for SI contractors, resident engineer's geotechnical assistant.

### 2.2 Johor and Malaysia
- JS-SEZ launched Jan 2025; Johor is APAC's largest data-centre market with a 5 GW+ pipeline (SE Asia had ~2.4 GW under construction in H1 2026). Every campus needs SI, piling supervision and load-test interpretation. RTS Link opens Dec 2026, making a Johor commute practical.
- PETRONAS CCS: HELMS Geomarine's 3+2-year offshore SI frame (June 2026) explicitly includes jack-up assessments, driveability and reporting. PETRONAS Carigali is tendering removal of up to 31 platforms; HELMS drilled in Sarawak waters in early 2026 for jack-up P&A campaigns.
- Floating solar: TNB Genco awarded a 595 MW Kenyir Lake project (construction from June 2026), Masdar signed a 200 MW Chereh Dam PPA, LSS6 (2 GW) expected mid-2026 with a larger floating share. Cirata in Indonesia needed ~2,500 anchors in very soft soil at up to 90 m depth: anchor design and lakebed SI are underserved skills.
- Slopes and landslides: Malaysian consultancies advertise slope hazard assessment and geoforensics; the AIAC 2026 arbitration rules and the 2024 amendment enlarge the dispute market.

**Roles he fits:** remote jack-up and driveability packages for HELMS and its competitors, decommissioning positioning assessments, data-centre SI and pile-test QC, floating-solar anchor SI, slope investigation.

### 2.3 Australia
- Bass Strait decommissioning (Allseas, 12 topsides, 11 jackets, lifts from 2027, ABL vessel suitability surveys); Northern Endeavour Phase 3a tender (14 Aug 2026).
- Jobs and Skills Australia lists geotechnical engineer as in shortage; national vacancy fill rate 68 % in March 2026. Australian consultancies hire remote contractors and sponsor migration.

### 2.4 Oil and gas, offshore
- Aramco restarting eight suspended jack-ups; Malaysia needs ~9 jack-ups in 2026; PETRONAS 2026 bid round; India's ONGC tender cancellations make India unpredictable.

### 2.5 Offshore wind survivors
- Fugro's FengMiao II SI (CIP, Taiwan) through Q3 2026; Ørsted Incheon surveys completed; Korea awarded 1.786 GW in H1 2026 and designates zones by end-2026; Japan Round 3 winners at Aomori South and Yuza.

### 2.6 Middle East
- Giga-projects are mixed: NEOM cancelled USD 6.85 bn of contracts in March 2026, but the regional geotechnical market is still growing and primes (AECOM, Bechtel) advertise geotechnical roles. Treat as an agency-placed option, not a direct target.

### 2.7 Subsea cables
- Singapore landings scheduled 2026–28 to SE Asia, the US/Guam, the Middle East and Australia; route surveys and burial assessments need client reps and reviewers.

## 4. Opportunities to explore (beyond 60 days)

1. **Professional Engineer (Civil) registration in Singapore, then Specialist PE (Geotechnical).** PEB updated the Specialist PE geotechnical guidelines in April 2026; the core competencies listed are SI and tests, pile load testing and interpretation, and instrumentation and monitoring, which he has. PE first (accredited degree, practical experience, PEB exams), then Specialist PE. This converts him from "engineer" to "signature", the difference between S$600 and S$1,500 days in Singapore. Cost is low; time is 12–24 months.
2. **Expert witness and forensic geotechnics.** SIAC, SICC and SOP-Act adjudications in Singapore, AIAC in Malaysia, plus insurers' loss adjusters on settlement, slope and foundation claims. Entry: list with expert-witness directories, write two short forensic case notes, take an expert-witness training course (e.g. SIArb or RICS). Pays well, no mobilisation.
3. **Floating-solar anchoring specialist for SEA.** Malaysia's LSS6, Kenyir, Chereh and Indonesia's reservoir programmes need lakebed SI and anchor design in soft soils, which is offshore mudmat and anchor thinking applied to reservoirs. Few people in the region have both.
4. **Decommissioning positioning specialist.** Malaysia's 31 platforms and Australia's Bass Strait need jack-up and heavy-lift site assessments next to old, sometimes scoured, foundations; a niche where his rig-move history is rare.
5. **Data-centre foundations in Johor.** Volume market; the winning play is a standing relationship with two or three piling contractors and one owner's engineer.
6. **Australia.** Register with Engineers Australia (CPEng or NER), take remote contract work first, consider sponsorship; geotechnical engineer is on the shortage list.
7. **Training and lecturing.** BCA Academy, polytechnics and NTU part-time modules on SI and offshore foundations; low pay, high credibility, useful for PE and expert-witness credentials.
8. **Lab accreditation consulting.** Regional SI contractors need SAC-SINGLAS or equivalent ISO/IEC 17025 scope to bid public work; he understands the tests and the reporting. Small, repeatable, remote.
9. **Own analysis products** (see `collaboration_assessment.md`): leg-penetration and report automation once he is billing again.
