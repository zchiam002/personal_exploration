|---|---|

| [opportunity_radar.py](opportunity_radar.py) | Daily digest of geotechnical work triggers (awards, campaigns, rig moves, decommissioning, CCS, cables, reclamation, tunnels, data centres, floating solar, disputes) from ten industry feeds, scored for his profile and reach. `python3 opportunity_radar.py --days 14` |
