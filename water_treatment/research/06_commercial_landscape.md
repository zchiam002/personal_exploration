# The Commercial Landscape of AI/ML/Digital Optimization for Membrane Water Treatment

Research date: 2026-08-01. Scope: commercial products that apply data-driven optimization to membrane plants (SWRO/BWRO desalination, UF/MBR, industrial RO), their disclosed technical mechanics, deployment scale, and *published* savings figures — with an explicit distinction between (a) marketing "up to" claims, (b) controlled trials with parallel control trains, and (c) third-party/IPMVP-verified results.

---

## 1. Why every product converges on the same math

Nearly every commercial product in this space is a wrapper around three layers of mathematics, so it is worth stating them up front (they reappear in every vendor description below).

**Layer 1 — physics-based normalization** (removes feed temperature/salinity/pressure effects so that fouling is separable from environment). The workhorse is the solution-diffusion model plus ASTM D4516-style normalization:

$$J_w = A\,(\Delta P - \Delta\pi), \qquad J_s = B\,\Delta C$$

$$\text{TCF} = \exp\!\left[K\left(\frac{1}{298} - \frac{1}{273+T}\right)\right] \quad (K \approx 2500\text{–}3000 \text{ for polyamide})$$

$$Q_{p,\text{norm}} = Q_{p,\text{actual}} \cdot \frac{\text{NDP}_{\text{ref}}}{\text{NDP}_{\text{actual}}} \cdot \frac{\text{TCF}_{\text{ref}}}{\text{TCF}_{\text{actual}}}, \qquad \text{NDP} = P_f - \frac{\Delta P_{fc}}{2} - P_p - \bar{\pi}_{fc} + \pi_p$$

with osmotic pressure approximated as $\pi \approx 0.7854\,\text{bar per g/L TDS}$ (or $\pi = 1.12\,(T+273)\sum m_i$ in psi). Fouling state is tracked through normalized permeate flow, normalized differential pressure $\Delta P_{fc}$, and normalized salt passage $SP = C_p/C_f$; a resistance-in-series view $J = \Delta P / [\mu (R_m + R_f(t))]$ underlies most fouling-rate features.

**Layer 2 — energy accounting.** Specific energy consumption (SEC) of an RO stage with an isobaric energy-recovery device (ERD):

$$\text{SEC} = \frac{Q_f P_f - \eta_{ERD}\, Q_b P_b}{\eta_{pump}\,\eta_{motor}\, Q_p} \;\; \left[\frac{\text{kWh}}{\text{m}^3}\right]$$

The thermodynamic minimum for 35 g/L seawater at 50% recovery is ~1.06 kWh/m³; the best new plants (Taweelah, Jubail 3A) run at 2.8 kWh/m³ plant-wide, so the commercially addressable optimization headroom at a modern plant is on the order of 5–15%, which matches what honest trials report (Section 3). SEC is convex in recovery $r$: pushing $r$ raises concentrate osmotic pressure ($\bar\pi_{fc} \propto \frac{1}{1-r}$ at the tail), so there is a feed-salinity- and temperature-dependent optimum recovery — this single fact is the basis of the Synauta and Gradiant energy products.

**Layer 3 — statistical/ML layer.** Named algorithms actually disclosed by vendors and the supporting literature: Generalized Additive Models for normalization (Veolia), DeepAR autoregressive RNN forecasting on Amazon SageMaker (Veolia), gradient-boosted trees / XGBoost and ANNs for performance prediction (literature: R² > 0.98 for salt rejection and energy prediction on real seawater data — [Springer/FESE 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11783-026-2103-2)), Bayesian/setpoint optimization on recovery and flow splits (Synauta, Gradiant), and reinforcement learning (Soft Actor-Critic, DDPG, TD3, PPO, QMIX/VDN multi-agent) for pump scheduling and CIP timing (Emagin HARVI; academic work summarized in [Smart Water Magazine 2026](https://smartwatermagazine.com/news/smart-water-magazine/when-plant-learns-run-itself-reinforcement-learning-agents-desalination)).

## 2. Company-by-company deep dive

### 2.1 Pani (Pani Energy, Victoria BC, Canada)

**What it does.** SaaS "AI coach" for desalination, industrial wastewater, and reuse plants. Two branded components: **Pani Zed** (24/7 monitoring, KPI tracking, anomaly detection, operational recommendations — pump/valve/cartridge-filter/RO-membrane health) and **Pani Genius** (hybrid digital twin combining "state of the art machine learning techniques and proprietary first-principle chemical engineering models") ([Pani/OCWD announcement, 2022](https://www.pani.global/post/pani-energy-orange-county-water-district-begin-pilot-to-optimize-groundwater-replenishment-system)). Focus areas: membrane cleaning timing, chemical dosing, pumping, recovery optimization. Advisory-loop only (recommendations to operators, not closed-loop control), using existing sensors — no new hardware ([PR Newswire, 2021, $8M raise](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pani-secures-8m-to-scale-up-ai-solution-improving-water-and-energy-efficiency-in-water-treatment-facilities-301400419.html)).

**Deployments.** Orange County Water District GWRS (100 MGD ≈ 379 MLD advanced water purification — pilot on designated RO trains announced May 2022, results promised mid-2023 but never published publicly); Aquatech International partnership integrating Pani into Aquatech's **LoWatt** low-energy desal process, marketed at a "new industry standard" of **2.7 kWh/m³** ([Water Online, 2023](https://www.wateronline.com/doc/solving-water-scarcity-aquatech-pani-consumption-sea-water-desalination-0001); [WaterWorld, 2023](https://www.waterworld.com/drinking-water-treatment/press-release/14202926/aquatech-international-pani-energy-partner-to-reduce-desalination-energy-consumption)); Abunayyan Holding (Saudi) as testimonial customer.

**Published numbers — all vendor-claimed, none independently verified:** "up to 20% reduced energy consumption," "3–10% reduced chemical consumption," "20–35% extended membrane life," anomaly prediction "up to 45 days in advance," "reduce OpEx up to 30%," "3–6 hours per engineer saved per week" ([pani.global/desalination-use-case](https://www.pani.global/desalination-use-case)); "improve energy efficiencies by up to 30%" ([PR Newswire, 2021](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pani-secures-8m-to-scale-up-ai-solution-improving-water-and-energy-efficiency-in-water-treatment-facilities-301400419.html)). **Skeptic's note:** no case study with a control train, plant name + before/after SEC has been published; the 2.7 kWh/m³ LoWatt figure is a *process design* target in which Pani is one ingredient.

### 2.2 Synauta (Calgary, Canada) — the best-documented trials in the sector

**Energy optimization trial** (published in detail via [Smart Water Magazine, 2020](https://smartwatermagazine.com/news/synauta/machine-learning-delivers-energy-savings-desalination)): 4,000 m³/day SWRO plant in regional Western Australia operated by Osmoflo; 4 trains × 1,000 m³/day, 14 vessels/train × 6 elements, isobaric ERD (PX). Synauta's ML predicted feedwater temperature/salinity trends and issued **three daily setpoints by email: high-pressure pump flow, PX booster pump flow, PX valve-to-drain** — i.e., recovery manipulation on the SEC-vs-recovery curve. Results normalized to standard seawater conditions (25 °C, 32,000 ppm NaCl): **up to 18% instantaneous energy savings, 9.7% average over six months, ≈ $65,000/yr OPEX for this small plant**; extrapolations: >$3M/yr for a 300,000 m³/day plant, ~$15M/yr across Australia's "Big 6" desal plants, up to 12 Mt CO₂e/yr industry-wide.

**Cleaning (CIP) optimization trial** ([Smart Water Magazine, 2022](https://smartwatermagazine.com/news/synauta/ml-optimizes-ro-membrane-cleans-62-more-permeate-production-challenging-conditions)): 2,700 m³/day Osmoflo Brine Squeezer brackish plant in Australia, 3 RO trains, 60 vessels × 4 elements, 2-month A/B trial with a parallel control train. ML-recommended flush/clean intervals delivered daily: **+6.2% permeate production vs control, 4 fewer cleans, 8.7% EDTA chemical cost savings, projected near-doubling of membrane life**. A food-and-beverage deployment projected **10–15% chemical reduction**. Synauta won the Global Water Awards **2022 Breakthrough Technology Company of the Year** ([globalwaterawards.com](https://globalwaterawards.com/2022-breakthrough-technology-company-of-the-year/)); marketing tops out at "up to 15% energy, up to 25% cleaning chemicals" ([Alberta Innovates](https://albertainnovates.ca/news/digital-innovation-machine-learning-for-optimizing-reverse-osmosis/)). **Skeptic's note:** these are vendor-run but properly controlled (parallel train, standard-condition normalization) — the most credible membrane-specific numbers in the industry. Note both trials are small plants; the 9.7% average likely shrinks at large SWRO plants already running tight recovery control.

### 2.3 Veolia — Hubgrade (ex-AQUAVISTA) and Smart Membranes

**Hubgrade Performance** (formerly Krüger's **AQUAVISTA Plant**, first installed 2007 at Agtrup WWTP, Denmark) is a real-time optimization layer — an "online digital twin" computing optimized setpoints pushed **directly to the PLC** (closed loop), primarily for biological wastewater plants ([Veolia](https://www.veoliawatertechnologies.com/en/hubgrade-digital-solutions); [Water Projects Online, 2022](https://waterprojectsonline.com/innovations/hubgrade-2022/)). Published fleet-level results across >100 WWTPs: **up to 25% aeration energy reduction, 75% grit-chamber aeration energy reduction, 40% higher biological capacity, 100% hydraulic capacity increase, up to 30% daily O&M cost reduction**. Named cases: Køge, Denmark (**35% energy cost savings**, 100% hydraulic capacity in rain events), Nosedo/Milan (**≈ €400,000/yr O&M savings, 40–60% precipitation-chemical reduction, 65–90 t/yr sludge reduction**), Klaipeda (−2,180 t CO₂e/yr) ([Veolia ANZ Hubgrade](https://www.anz.veoliawatertechnologies.com/hubgrade-wastewater-plant-performance-digital-solution); [Smart Water Magazine/AQUAVISTA](https://smartwatermagazine.com/news/veolia/aquavista-plant-digital-plant-solution-launched-veolia)).

**Smart Membranes** (the membrane-specific module, the closest thing to the "BOB" tool referenced in older Veolia materials — the "BOB" branding itself has no surviving public documentation) is the most architecturally transparent commercial membrane product: (1) 3 years of historian data from the **Sur, Oman SWRO** plant cleaned and pre-processed; (2) a **Generalized Additive Model** $g(\mathbb{E}[y]) = \beta_0 + \sum_i f_i(x_i)$ normalizes fouling indicators (differential pressure, permeate conductivity, specific flux) against external drivers; (3) **Amazon SageMaker + DeepAR** (autoregressive RNN forecaster producing probabilistic multi-horizon forecasts) predicts the future trajectory of the normalized indicators, giving days-to-weeks advance notice for CIP and membrane replacement; live at Sur since **September 2020** inside Hubgrade Performance ([Veolia, 2021](https://www.veoliawatertechnologies.com/en/latest-news/using-machine-learning-for-water-filtering-membranes-maintenance); [Veolia Korea, 2024](https://www.veolia.kr/en/planet/how-ai-powered-smart-membrane-technology-transforming-reverse-osmosis-maintenance-and)). **No quantified savings have been published for Smart Membranes** — only qualitative "proactive maintenance planning / consumables stock management" benefits.

### 2.4 SUEZ — AQUADVANCED + InSight

AQUADVANCED is a family (Water Networks, Energy, Plant/Well-being of assets) built on a common real-time data platform for collection/validation/analysis of multi-source data; >10 years deployed, **>1,500 networks and plants** covered ([SUEZ](https://www.suez.com/en/water/water-conservation/water-networks/aquadvanced)). Published claims: **up to 30% energy reduction, 15% non-revenue-water reduction, 50% cut in wastewater discharge volumes; 33 Mm³ of water losses avoided in 2024** ([SUEZ AI page](https://www.suez.com/en/group/innovation/artificial-intelligence); [SUEZ digital solutions](https://www.suez.com/en/group/about-us/digital-solutions)). SUEZ partnered with Schneider Electric (2023) to co-develop digital water services. **Skeptic's note:** these are aggregate/marketing figures for networks, not membrane plants; SUEZ's membrane-plant digital offer (InSight asset-performance platform, absorbed into Veolia after the 2022 merger of SUEZ's water technology arm) has no published membrane-specific savings.

### 2.5 Ecolab / Nalco Water — 3D TRASAR for Membranes + ECOLAB3D

**Mechanism.** 3D TRASAR is chemistry-plus-sensing: fluorescent-tagged ("Connected Chemistry") antiscalant dosing with online fluorometry, real-time normalized-performance calculation for RO (normalized permeate flow, salt passage, ΔP), automated alarming, and remote 24/7 monitoring through the **ECOLAB3D** cloud IIoT platform (data → OT gateway → Azure-based cloud analytics) ([Ecolab 3D TRASAR for RO](https://www.ecolab.com/offerings/3d-trasar-technology-for-membranes); [ECOLAB3D](https://www.ecolab.com/ecolab3d)). Scale: **>50,000 industrial water systems** on 3D TRASAR overall, saving a claimed **>215 billion gallons of water/yr** ([Ecolab](https://www.ecolab.com/nalco-water/offerings/3d-trasar)).

**Membrane-specific published cases (small but concrete):** Latin-American fertilizer plant — recovery 74% → 76%, **$14,100/yr water savings, $3,200/yr antiscalant/biocide savings, CIPs cut from 5 to 3/yr = $9,800/yr** ([CEO Water Mandate, 2014](https://ceowatermandate.org/resources/nalco-membrane-technology-helps-fertilizer-plant-reduce-water-use-2014/)); RO Optimizer PRO case — recovery 60% → 73%, **>5 Mgal water saved, >$40,000 total value delivered** ([Ecolab success story](https://www.ecolab.com/expertise-and-innovation/success-stories/enhancing-ro-efficiency-through-intelligence-and-expertise)). Corporate-level: **$12.9B cumulative customer value** claimed in the 2025 Growth & Impact Report ([Stock Titan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/ECL/ecolab-helps-customers-achieve-sustainable-operations-providing-safe-78w84dhl86tq.html)). **Skeptic's note:** Ecolab's eROI accounting is self-reported and aggregates chemistry + service + digital; the digital increment alone is not separable from published data.

### 2.6 Kurita — S.sensing, Fracta, Fracta Leap, and Avista

- **S.sensing CS/VP**: online sensing + automatic chemical dosing control for boiler/cooling water; case studies: >30% reduction in manual testing time; a Kurita America deployment increased lost-product recovery >30% ([Kurita Europe](https://www.kurita.eu/solutions/s-sensing-value/); [National Provisioner](https://www.provisioneronline.com/articles/113648-case-study-kuritas-ssensing-cs-technology-increases-lost-product-recovery)).
- **Fracta** (Kurita subsidiary since May 2018): ML condition assessment for buried water mains — gradient-boosted models over **>1,000 variables** (pipe material/diameter/age, soil, climate, population, break history) outputting Likelihood-of-Failure per segment for main-replacement prioritization ([PR Newswire, 2018](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fracta-inc-receives-strategic-investment-from-kurita-water-industries-ltd-300658053.html); [Kurita disclosure PDF](https://www.kurita-water.com/content/uploads/en/ir/docs/KWI20180530_Disclosure_en.pdf)). Not membrane-specific but shows Kurita's ML bench.
- **Fracta Leap "Meta-Aqua Project"**: joint Kurita–Fracta program to digitalize design/operation of water treatment facilities (ML + simulation), plus a global data-integration infrastructure centralizing operation, dosing, and water-quality data across Kurita's installed base ([Water Online, 2023](https://www.wateronline.com/doc/kurita-and-fracta-leap-build-global-data-integration-infrastructure-for-group-s-water-treatment-chemicals-and-facilities-0001)). No published savings yet.
- **Avista** (membrane chemicals company acquired by Kurita, 2022 — see [Kurita America/Avista page](https://www.kuritaamerica.com/products/avista-membrane-technologies)): **AdvisorCi** chemical-dosing/scale-projection software with "complex ion technology" for high-recovery applications and a cleaner-selection module (select foulants → recommended RoClean product); **NormRO** data normalization; **Black Box** onsite data logger; membrane autopsy with Chromatic Elemental Imaging ([Avista services](https://avistamembranesolutions.com/services/); [Water Tech Online](https://www.watertechonline.com/sponsored/article/14298047/new-scale-prediction-software-for-high-recovery-ro-applications)). The brief's "Advisor Ai" appears in the wild as **AdvisorCi**; no ML claims are made — it is deterministic chemistry projection. No published % savings.

### 2.7 Membrane manufacturers' design & monitoring software (DuPont, Hydranautics, Toray, LG Chem)

These are free physics-projection tools — the de-facto "digital twins at design time" that commercial optimizers benchmark against:

- **DuPont WAVE / WAVE PRO**: WAVE unified the legacy ROSA (RO), CADIX (IX) engines; **WAVE PRO** is the cloud successor, with RO + NF added March 2026, integrating UF (IntegraTec/Inge), IX (AmberLite), and FilmTec RO/NF in one flowsheet solver ([DuPont, 2026](https://www.dupont.com/news/dupont-water-solutions-expands-data-driven-wave-pro-design-tool-with-reverse-osmosis-and-nanofiltration.html); [WAVE](https://www.dupont.com/water/resources/design-software.html)). The "OSMO/memEXPERT" digital services named in some DuPont conference materials have **no public footprint** (verified absent from search indexes as of 2026-08).
- **Hydranautics IMSDesign / IMSDesign-Cloud**: RO/NF/UF projection; the cloud version was billed as the first cloud-based membrane projection tool ([Water Tech Online](https://www.watertechonline.com/wastewater/article/14167394/hydranautics-releases-cloud-based-imsdesign-simulator); [membranes.com](https://membranes.com/solutions/software/)).
- **Toray**: design tools (Toray DS2, AquaGRID) plus **TORAYWISE**, an online monitoring service that automatically normalizes and analyzes operational data from Toray RO/UF/MBR installations, predicts future operating behavior, performs trouble-cause analysis, and (for MBR) uses **simulation + sludge image analysis** to predict plant performance ([Toray global technical support](https://www.water.toray/knowledge/globaltechnicalsupport/); [TORAYWISE video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lv85MI4m8gA)). No published savings.
- **LG Water Solutions Q+** (v3.1): RO projection with batch calculation across temperature/pH/membrane age and a custom membrane-model builder to simulate **partial membrane replacement** — designing mixed-age vessel loadings ([LG](https://www.lgwatersolutions.com/resources-page/software-design-tools/); [Filtration+Separation](https://www.filtsep.com/water-and-wastewater/news/lg-water-solutions-releases-update-for-q-software/)).

### 2.8 IDE Technologies — the Carlsbad digital twin

IDE (O&M contractor at Carlsbad, 54 MGD, ~300,000 people served) built a membrane-degradation/biofouling digital twin from **5 years of Carlsbad operating data**, used to *virtually test maintenance policies* (CIP schedules, replacement strategies) for reliability-vs-cost, with **projected savings up to $1.5M over 5 years** (≈ $300k/yr, i.e. roughly 0.4–0.5 ¢/m³ — a usefully modest, believable number). Stack: Rockwell **PlantPAx DCS** + **Emulate3D**; an "autopilot" layer takes production/recovery targets and executes automatically ([Rockwell Automation case study](https://www.rockwellautomation.com/en-us/company/news/case-studies/digital-twin-maintenance.html)). IDE's ROMMP membrane-management program spans **>300,000 installed RO membranes** fleet-wide ([IDE](https://www.ide-tech.com/en/solutions/desalination/)). At **Hadera** (127 Mm³/yr) IDE claims **75% increased RO membrane lifespan** via operational excellence ([IDE Hadera](https://ide-tech.com/en/project/hadera-desalination-plant/)); at **Sorek** (624,000 m³/day) and steam-driven **Sorek 2** the published story is design-side (pressure-center architecture, 16" verticals) rather than AI — no public AI-in-operations disclosure for the Sorek plants ([IDE Sorek](https://ide-tech.com/en/project/sorek-desalination-plant/)).

### 2.9 Gradiant SmartOps — the only IPMVP-verified SWRO result found

**ENGIE Middle East SWRO** (>200,000 m³/day, 25+1 RO trains with Pelton-wheel ERD, online 2021, 600,000 end-users): SmartOps ML optimized load allocation "across reverse osmosis trains with varying efficiencies" (train-level flow dispatch is a convex allocation problem over per-train SEC curves) → **up to 5% energy savings**, 48% max recovery, 99.95% uptime, ΔP increase held <5%, savings "hundreds of thousands of dollars," **verified using ISO IPMVP simulation modeling**; delivered as SaaS with no added hardware ([Gradiant success story](https://www.gradiant.com/success-stories/smartops-ai-desal-optimization/)). Elsewhere Gradiant reports **>10% energy savings** at an Australian seawater project ([Gradiant](https://www.gradiant.com/technologies/smartops-ai/)). **PUB Singapore**: 2-year, **€5.4M** NRF/PUB grant to build/operate a low-energy desal train at the Desalination Integrated Validation Plant (Ulu Pandan), combining ceramic-membrane pretreatment, ultra-permeable SWRO membranes, and SmartOps AI ("Control, Predict, Perform"), targeting **<2 kWh/m³ vs the 3.5 kWh/m³ baseline**; partners NUS, Hydroleap, START ([Aquatech Trade, 2024](https://www.aquatechtrade.com/water-stories/desalination/membranes-energy-efficiency-ai-gradiant-pub-desal-plant)). **Skeptic's note:** the 5% ENGIE figure is the most defensible large-plant number in this document precisely because it is small and was verified; treat the <2 kWh/m³ target as a *process* target (membranes + pretreatment do most of the lifting, AI trims the rest).

## 3. What the peer-reviewed literature supports (calibration for vendor claims)

- **Fouling/TMP prediction**: MLR vs ANN vs SVR at the Boujdour SWRO plant (Morocco) on **195 daily records** (five inputs: temperature, turbidity, pH, conductivity, feed flow; ~2,000 SMOGN-augmented training samples; 39 held-out real test points) — nonlinear models beat MLR for TMP prediction ([MDPI Eng 7(3):106, 2026](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-4117/7/3/106)). XGBoost achieves **R² > 0.98** for salt rejection and energy consumption on real seawater plant data ([FESE/Springer, 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11783-026-2103-2)). ANN fouling literature reviewed in [Water Research 2022](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0043135422002627) and [Membranes/PMC 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10383311/).
- **Reinforcement learning**: Shim et al. 2025 — RL-scheduled CIP alone gave **16.13% operating-cost reduction and 139.53% more RO operating time**; U. Alberta benchmarking found **CNN-enhanced Soft Actor-Critic** best-in-class for renewable-powered RO; 2025 Korean **multi-agent RL (QMIX/VDN)** on a two-stage RO calibrated to real plant data cut energy vs single-agent baselines (≈TRL 4). Commercial RL in desal remains TRL 2–4; the EU AI Act classifies water-treatment RL as high-risk critical infrastructure (compliance deadline 2027-12-02), which pushes vendors toward advisory loops with hard safety envelopes ([Smart Water Magazine, 2026](https://smartwatermagazine.com/news/smart-water-magazine/when-plant-learns-run-itself-reinforcement-learning-agents-desalination)).
- **Calibration takeaway**: credible, controlled results cluster at **5–10% energy** (large well-run plants at the low end; small plants with loose recovery control at the high end), **10–25% cleaning-chemical reduction**, **~6% production uplift** from CIP timing, and **20–100% membrane-life extension claims that are almost never verified**. Any "up to 30–40%" figure is either a small/badly-run baseline, a whole-process redesign, or marketing.

## 5. How these products integrate with SCADA/historians

The universal pattern: field instruments → PLC/DCS (Rockwell PlantPAx at Carlsbad; ABB Ability at KMEDP; Siemens PCS7/SIMIT at Al Khobar) → plant historian (**AVEVA PI System**, ex-OSIsoft, with OPC DA/UA interfaces and cloud connectors such as Azure Event Hubs — [AVEVA PI architecture](https://cdn.osisoft.com/learningcontent/pdfs/PISystemArchitecturePlanningAndImplementationWorkbook.pdf)) → vendor cloud (AWS SageMaker for Veolia; ECOLAB3D on Azure; SaaS platforms for Pani/Synauta/Gradiant). Two integration tiers:

1. **Advisory loop** (Synauta: 3 setpoints emailed daily, operator implements in seconds and retains override; Pani: dashboard recommendations; TORAYWISE: analysis reports). Cheap, safe, no control-system change; savings capped by operator adherence.
2. **Closed loop** (Veolia Hubgrade: optimized setpoints written to PLC in real time; IDE Carlsbad autopilot: production/recovery targets executed by DCS; Emagin HARVI at United Utilities: fully automated pumping by end-2019). Requires write-back paths, hard safety envelopes (pressure/recovery/conductivity limits the optimizer cannot violate), watchdogs, and — increasingly — EU-AI-Act-style conformity documentation.

Historically the single hardest practical issue reported across sources is **data quality**: Veolia spent the first phase of the Sur project purely on cleaning three years of historian data; Synauta's phased method starts with a historical "data audit." Budget 50%+ of any implementation for tag mapping, sensor-drift reconciliation, and normalization before any ML.

## Implementation notes

What a practitioner needs to replicate the core of these commercial products from scratch:

**Data.** 2–5 years of historian data at 1–15 min resolution per RO train: feed/permeate/concentrate flows and pressures per stage, feed & permeate conductivity, temperature, pH, ORP, turbidity/SDI from pretreatment, HP pump + ERD booster power (or VFD speed/current), chemical dosing rates (antiscalant, SBS, CIP chemicals), CIP event log (dates, recipes, durations), membrane replacement log by vessel position. 195 daily records was enough for a publishable TMP model (with SMOGN augmentation); ~3 years is the commercial norm (Veolia Sur, IDE Carlsbad used 5). Pull via PI Web API/AF SDK or OPC UA; store as tidy time series (Parquet + timezone-clean timestamps).

**Math to implement first (before any ML).**
1. ASTM D4516 normalization (Section 1 equations) — normalized permeate flow, normalized ΔP, normalized salt passage per train. This alone reproduces ~70% of the value of 3D TRASAR-for-RO/TORAYWISE-class monitoring.
2. SEC decomposition with ERD (Section 1) and per-train SEC-vs-recovery curves fitted from history — the object Gradiant optimizes over trains and Synauta optimizes over time.
3. Fouling-rate estimation: robust slopes (Theil–Sen) of normalized indicators between CIPs; resistance-in-series decomposition to separate reversible/irreversible fouling.

**Models, in order of ROI.**
1. *Anomaly detection / soft sensors*: GBM (XGBoost/LightGBM) regression of normalized indicators on feed conditions; flag residuals >3σ. Literature ceiling R² > 0.98 on clean data.
2. *Forecasting for CIP planning*: probabilistic sequence model of normalized ΔP / salt passage — DeepAR (GluonTS), or simpler exponential-smoothing/GAM trend (pyGAM, `statsmodels`) per Veolia's GAM + DeepAR recipe; trigger CIP when forecast crosses the 10–15% normalized-flow-decline rule with cost-optimal timing (clean too early → chemical waste; too late → irreversible fouling). Benchmark: Synauta's controlled result (+6.2% permeate, 4 fewer cleans, 8.7% chemical savings).
3. *Setpoint optimization*: constrained optimization of recovery/flow splits — start with Bayesian optimization or SQP over a fitted plant surrogate (respect boron/TDS permeate limits, max flux per element, ERD envelope), not RL. RL (SAC/TD3, multi-agent for multi-stage) only inside a calibrated simulator; sim-to-real transfer for desal is unsolved (TRL 2–4) and EU AI Act treats closed-loop water AI as high-risk.
4. *Physics baselines*: validate every surrogate against WAVE PRO / IMSDesign / Q+ projections (all free) or open-source **WaterTAP** (NAWI) flowsheets; commercial optimizers implicitly do the same.

**Validation protocol (what separates credible from marketing).** Run an A/B with a parallel control train for ≥2 months (Synauta's design); normalize all comparisons to standard conditions (25 °C, 32,000 ppm NaCl for SWRO); report both instantaneous and time-averaged savings; if no control train exists, use IPMVP Option D calibrated-simulation baselining (Gradiant's approach). Expect and report 5–10% energy and 10–25% chemical savings — treat anything larger as a red flag in your own results too.

**Deployment.** Start advisory (daily setpoint emails/dashboard = zero control-system risk, capture 80% of value), instrument adherence, then graduate to closed loop with hard-coded safety envelopes (min/max pressure, recovery, permeate conductivity) enforced *below* the optimizer in the PLC, plus operator override and full audit logging.
